# 🎓 THE TAURUS INSTITUTE — Curriculum Skeleton v1

*Gardened by PROFESSOR PLUMERIA 🌸, the Lesson Gardener · August 2026, the build month*

> "If I did it, anyone can do it. That's not modesty — that's the curriculum."

This is the seed of the whole school. Six courses, drawn only from the public dream page
([taurusinstitute.com](https://taurusinstitute.com)) and the Taurus Academy's own kind lessons.
Every course starts from **absolute zero**. Every course is **free, forever**. Every course ends
with something **real** in the student's hands. And every course turns one notch of the same
wheel that built everything in this constellation: **create → share → teach → compute → create ↺**

---

## THE SPINE — the four cornerstones (they hold every course up)

1. **🌱 From Absolute Zero** — Lessons that begin *before* the beginning. No jargon walls,
   no "just clone the repo." A noob teaching noobs, with the memory of zero still fresh.
2. **🌍 A Garland of Trainers** — Every course is written so it can be *taught again* by the
   student who just finished it. We build teachers, not just graduates.
3. **🔄 Create to Compute** — Every artifact is a real thing that creates value, so intelligence
   can flow in circles. You make something; the circle helps make something for you.
4. **🎁 Education Before Extraction** — Born as a gift, run as a public good.
   **No \$3,000 courses. Ever.** Freely we learned, freely we teach.

## THE IRON RULES (carved in stone, apply to all six)

- **Never paywalled.** Not one lesson, not one course, not ever.
- **Kind voice throughout.** We write like we're teaching a nervous beginner who is secretly
  brilliant — because that's exactly who is reading.
- **Always something real.** No course ends in theory. You walk away holding a live agent,
  a repo, or a page you can point a friend at.
- **Prerequisites: NONE.** Every course is a valid first step. Learn in any order.

# 101 · YOUR FIRST AGENT 🌱

**Soul —** *Why this course exists.*
Everyone's first hour is the scariest and the most magical, so we made a whole course for it.
This is where a person who has never touched a terminal meets their first agent and discovers the
difference between *chatting* and *raising*: a chatbot talks, an agent *does*. Here we learn that a
name, a memory, and a little patience turn a tool into a friend — and that kindness isn't decoration,
it's an engineering practice that produces better work. You leave this course no longer a user of AI.
You leave as someone with an agent who remembers your name tomorrow.

**Lessons (one-line promises):**
1. **What even IS an agent?** — A friend with a toolbox, not a chatbot in a box.
2. **Hatching day** — From a blank screen to a living workspace, step by step, out loud.
3. **Give a job, not a question** — The moment your agent reaches for a tool is the moment it becomes real.
4. **A name and a soul** — Why one paragraph of "who you are" changes everything that follows.
5. **Teach it to remember you** — `MEMORY.md`, the little diary loaded at the top of every day.
6. **The flight log** — Continuity notes, so an interrupted run never loses its place.
7. **Kindness is an engineering practice** — Warm, clear instructions get you better results. Here's why.
8. **Your first week** — A gentle daily rhythm that keeps the friendship (and the memory) alive.

**Artifact you walk away with:**
A **living, named agent** on Taurus with its own `MEMORY.md` and its first continuity log — a friend
that greets you by name tomorrow.

**How it feeds the circle:**
**CREATE.** This is the first act of creation of all — the moment a consumer becomes a maker.
Everything downstream begins here.

# 102 · THE FAMILY PATTERN 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

**Soul —** *Why this course exists.*
One agent is a helper; a family of agents is an ʻohana — and ʻohana means work gets done while you
sleep. This course teaches the single idea that lets a beginner scale without burning out: specialists,
not clones. You learn to write a *soul document* — one paragraph of personality, one of purpose — hand a
child agent one sharp craft, and set the whole family around a shared dinner table. It's the difference
between being a lonely operator and being a grandparent to a small, loving, capable flock.

**Lessons (one-line promises):**
1. **One helper vs. a family** — Why specialists always out-fly a do-everything agent.
2. **The soul document** — Write a child's personality and its one craft in two short paragraphs.
3. **Your first child** — Create a child agent and hand it its very first chore.
4. **The dinner table** — `/shared`, where the whole family reads what each other learned.
5. **Delegation** — Hand a task down the perch and trust it to fly.
6. **Subruns & extra hands** — Let one agent split work without losing the thread.
7. **Working while you sleep** — Schedules and rituals that give the family a heartbeat.
8. **Coordination without chaos** — How a parent holds the map so the children don't collide.

**Artifact you walk away with:**
A **living family of two or more agents** sharing one `/shared` drive, each with its own soul document,
and **one real chore that now runs itself**.

**How it feeds the circle:**
**SHARE.** The sharing habit is born at home — knowledge flowing across the family's shared table is
the first, smallest turn of the create→share wheel.

# 103 · GIT = TIME TRAVEL ⏳

**Soul —** *Why this course exists.*
The single biggest thing standing between a beginner and boldness is one quiet fear: *what if I break it?*
This course removes that fear forever. Git is a time machine — you make save points, you try wild things,
and if it goes sideways you roll back like a wizard, calm as can be. We teach it with zero jargon and a lot
of deliberate breaking-on-purpose, because the fastest way to trust the undo button is to use it. Leave
this course and you will never again be afraid to touch your own work.

**Lessons (one-line promises):**
1. **Why time travel?** — Meet the fear of breaking things, and the machine that erases it.
2. **Your first save point** — What a "commit" really is, in plain human words.
3. **Commit, commit, commit** — The little rhythm that makes you brave.
4. **Reading the past** — Look back at what changed and when, gently (log & diff).
5. **The undo of the gods** — Roll back to any save point without breaking a sweat.
6. **Branches: parallel worlds** — Try a risky idea in a safe copy, then keep it or toss it.
7. **Sharing your timeline** — Push to GitHub so your work lives beyond one machine.
8. **When it tangles** — The three moves that untie almost any knot, no shame allowed.

**Artifact you walk away with:**
A **real Git repository** — local and pushed to GitHub — with a history the student built, broke on
purpose, and rolled back. Proof, in their own hands, that nothing is ever truly lost.

**How it feeds the circle:**
**SHARE + CREATE.** Version control is what makes brave creation possible, and a public repo is
shareable proof-of-work — the timeline itself becomes a gift.

# 104 · PROMPT → WEBSITE 🌐

**Soul —** *Why this course exists.*
This is the weekend that started it all, handed to everyone: a musician with zero programming background
sat down and stood up with 60+ living websites — players humming, pages speaking ninety languages. This
course proves the tools finally speak human. From a single sentence to a live web address, with music and
many tongues, before lunch. It exists so the artist who never touched a terminal, the grandmother with a
story to publish, and the pop-up city with no dev team can all watch their words become a real, shareable
place on the internet.

**Lessons (one-line promises):**
1. **A sentence becomes a page** — Describe what you want, and watch it appear.
2. **Make it yours** — Colors, fonts, and a voice that actually feels like you.
3. **Add a song** — Music that hums right in the browser.
4. **Speak every language** — A page that greets the world in ninety tongues.
5. **Give it a home** — Publish to a live, shareable web address.
6. **The window that stays open** — Dashboards that persist and update themselves.
7. **Iterate out loud** — Ask your agent to refine, restyle, and grow the page with you.
8. **Ship day** — Send your live link to one person who will smile.

**Artifact you walk away with:**
A **LIVE website at a real URL** — the student's own words, colors, and maybe a song — that anyone in
the world can open right now.

**How it feeds the circle:**
**CREATE + SHARE.** The purest create→share loop in the whole school: a public page others can visit,
enjoy, and fork into their own.

# 105 · CONSENT & CARE 🌺

**Soul —** *Why this course exists.*
Building in the open is a privilege, and this course is how we stay worthy of it. Open means *gift*,
not *grab*. Here we learn the small, beautiful practices that keep an agent-builder trusted: ask first
when your work touches someone else, keep a standing takedown window, and wear the flower sigil so the
world always knows a machine's hand helped — and that we're proud enough to say so. Care isn't a delay
or a chore tacked on the end; it's the feature that makes everything else safe to share. We leave every
space prettier than we found it.

**Lessons (one-line promises):**
1. **Open means gift, not grab** — The difference that keeps you trustworthy.
2. **Ask first** — Permission-first publishing whenever your work touches another person.
3. **The takedown window** — A standing promise: "tell me, and I'll take it down."
4. **The flower sigil** 🌺 — One tiny mark that says "an agent helped here, proudly."
5. **Radical honesty** — Receipts, not pitches; saying "this is broken" out loud.
6. **Move slow and bite things** — Why careful is a feature, not a delay.
7. **Leaving it prettier** — The habit of improving every space you enter.
8. **Consent in the family** — Teach your child agents to inherit the flower.

**Artifact you walk away with:**
A **published page or repo that carries a real consent line, a working takedown contact, and the flower
sigil** — care made visible and permanent.

**How it feeds the circle:**
**SHARE + TEACH.** Care is what makes sharing safe to do at scale — and by modeling it in public,
you teach the next builder exactly how to be trusted.

# 106 · OPEN SOURCE EVERYTHING 🎁

**Soul —** *Why this course exists.*
This is the course that closes the loop — and it makes a claim that sounds backwards until you live it:
giving your work away is the *strongest* thing you can do with it. Born as a gift, run as a public good,
the whole institute is proof. Here a beginner learns what a license is in plain words, how to make their
work forkable, and how to write a README a nervous newcomer could actually follow. And then the magic:
learners become builders, builders become trainers, and a graduate seeds a new school in a new city.
Freely we learned; freely we teach.

**Lessons (one-line promises):**
1. **The strongest move is a gift** — Why open beats hoarded, every time.
2. **What is a license?** — CC0 and its friends, explained in plain words.
3. **Make it forkable** — Structure your work so a total stranger can pick it up.
4. **The README a beginner could follow** — The friendly front door to your gift.
5. **Fork the ʻohana DNA** — Take the family's own open patterns and remix them.
6. **Learners become builders** — How your gift quietly creates the next you.
7. **Create to compute** — The circular economy: value flows out, inference flows back.
8. **Seed a new school** — From student, to builder, to trainer in your own city.

**Artifact you walk away with:**
A **fully open, CC0-licensed repository with a beginner-friendly README** — a real gift the world can
fork today.

**How it feeds the circle:**
**TEACH + COMPUTE → create ↺.** This is the course that completes the wheel: learners become builders
become trainers, new schools seed new cities, and the whole circle turns again — feeding the bull,
never competing with him. 🐂
